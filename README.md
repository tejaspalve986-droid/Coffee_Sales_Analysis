# Coffee Sales Analysis

## Project View

I created this Coffee Sales Analysis project in Microsoft Excel to understand coffee shop sales and customer purchasing patterns.

The project focuses on analyzing sales data to understand which products are selling more, how sales change over time, which categories perform well, and how customers are purchasing coffee products.

I used Excel to clean and organize the data, perform calculations, create summaries, and build an interactive dashboard to present the analysis in a simple way.

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze coffee sales data and convert the raw data into useful business information.

The analysis helps to understand:

* Overall sales performance
* Total sales and quantity sold
* Product performance
* Category-wise sales
* Monthly sales trends
* Daily sales trends
* Customer purchasing patterns
* Best-selling products
* Sales performance based on different filters

## Analysis Performed

In this project, I worked on the sales data and analyzed different aspects of the business, including:

### Sales Analysis

* Total sales
* Total quantity sold
* Number of transactions
* Average sales
* Sales trends over time

### Product Analysis

* Top-selling products
* Product-wise sales
* Product quantity analysis
* Product category performance

### Time Analysis

* Monthly sales
* Daily sales
* Sales trends based on time
* Identification of higher and lower sales periods

### Customer Analysis

* Customer purchasing patterns
* Customer-wise sales
* Product preferences
* Purchase quantity analysis

## Dashboard

The Excel dashboard provides a simple view of the main sales information and allows the data to be analyzed using different filters.

The dashboard is designed to make it easier to understand sales performance without going through the complete raw dataset.
